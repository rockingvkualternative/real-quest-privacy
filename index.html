REAL QUEST Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how REAL QUEST ("the App") handles information when you use the game. REAL QUEST is designed to provide fitness/gameplay features while minimizing the information it stores.

1. Information the App Processes

Motion and step sensor data: REAL QUEST may access supported Android motion sensors, such as the step detector, accelerometer, and gyroscope, to determine whether movement qualifies as a genuine walking step and to apply anti-cheat checks. Sensor readings are processed for gameplay and step verification. The App does not require you to enter health information manually.

Gameplay data: The App may store gameplay information such as verified steps, experience points, level progress, achievements, missions, inventory, and other game progress. Where supported, this information may be stored locally on your device.

Advertising and analytics: REAL QUEST uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google Firebase/Google Analytics services. These services may process information such as advertising identifiers, device/app information, diagnostics, approximate information, and interaction data as described by Google's applicable policies and your consent choices. Advertising and analytics providers may receive information directly from the App or device.

Important: REAL QUEST does not sell your personal information. The App does not intentionally collect your name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, or precise location unless a future version specifically introduces such a feature and updates this Privacy Policy.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To verify walking steps and detect invalid movement or shaking.
  • To maintain gameplay progress and provide game features.
  • To show advertisements and measure advertising performance.
  • To understand app performance and improve reliability.
  • To prevent abuse, cheating, duplicate events, and invalid gameplay activity.

3. Third-Party Services

REAL QUEST may use third-party services including:

  • Google AdMob — advertising and related measurement.
  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics — app analytics, diagnostics, and usage measurement.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws. For more information, visit Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising and Consent

REAL QUEST may display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Where legally required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage consent choices. Users may be presented with privacy choices depending on their location and applicable regulations.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored gameplay data can generally be removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. Data processed by third-party advertising or analytics providers is handled according to those providers' retention policies and applicable law.

6. Children's Privacy

REAL QUEST is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to the App, please contact us so that the issue can be reviewed.

7. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the App. However, no electronic system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or REAL QUEST's data practices, contact the developer through the support/contact information provided on the official app store listing.
